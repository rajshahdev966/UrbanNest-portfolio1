Studio History

From a Sketchbook to a Legacy

UrbanNest started as a small two-person architectural workspace. Over the past decade, we have established our signature warm minimalist aesthetic, blending raw organic textures with contemporary technology.

Today, our team of space architects, lighting experts, and furniture designers works in unison to craft high-performance residential estates worldwide.

2018

The Foundation

Founding the studio in a tiny rented workspace, focused on bespoke residential layout drafting.

2021

The Sustainable Pivot

Integrating carbon-neutral wall paints and eco-certified solid timber resources across all projects.

2026

Global Expansion

Inaugurating virtual layout design hubs to provide immersive photorealistic 3D VR tours to remote international clients.
